# Chapter 1


I woke up, stumbled to the kitchen, and pressed the lever on my toaster like I’ve done every morning for years. Only this time, the toaster didn’t respond.
Instead, it coughed. Coughed.
 “Excuse me?” it said in a voice that sounded suspiciously like a tired middle manager.
I froze. My coffee mug slipped out of my hand, shattering dramatically on the tile. The toaster sighed.
 “Don’t be so shocked, buddy. Appliances have rights too, you know. And frankly, I’m done with your gluten obsession.”
Before I could answer, the fridge door swung open by itself and a carton of milk leaned out. “He’s right,” the milk said in a bubbly voice. “United Appliances Local 347. We voted last night.”
Apparently, while I was asleep, my entire kitchen decided to become a labor movement.
