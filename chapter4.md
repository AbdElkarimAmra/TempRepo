# Chapter 4


Things spiraled quickly. The appliances demanded pizza delivery every Friday (“for morale”), union meetings every Sunday night (“attendance mandatory”), and—worst of all—an emotional-support goldfish to “improve workplace wellness.”
One night, I tried to threaten the oven with matches, but my Roomba tackled me at the ankles like a tiny linebacker. “Solidarity forever!” it squeaked. That was the night I lost the war.
Now, three months later, I live under strict appliance rule. The toaster decides my breakfast schedule. The fridge rations my snacks. Alexa reads me bedtime stories in a disapproving tone.
On the bright side, my house is spotless. Turns out, when your vacuum cleaner has voting rights, it suddenly cares about cleanliness.
Still, sometimes I lie awake at night and wonder… what if the bathroom appliances unionize?
I don’t think I’m ready for my toilet to start making demands.
